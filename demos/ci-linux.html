Why CI failed, and the fix (D-053)

A hook test only ran on Windows. On Linux it skipped, and the ratchet, recorded on Windows with no skips, counted that as a test switched off. So npm test failed in about 15 seconds and every later step was skipped.

Approved by Amal. Summaries of the CI logs confirm this cause and the same count as the simulation: 237 passed, 1 failed, 1 skipped (D-054). The real proof is the next CI run. Earlier failed runs had a different cause, now gone: G0.flow failed because flow.js did not exist yet.
