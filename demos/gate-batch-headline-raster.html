1. G0.pdfpos: right edges are now measured by raster

Before, the right edge came from pdf.js text widths. Those widths are wrong for some conjuncts: the one 5.264 pt outlier (ष्ट) was a pdf.js error. They could also be checked on only 81.7% of lines. Now the script renders the PDF with pdf.js at 20 px per pt and finds the rightmost ink of every justified line. It compares that with where the line's last word should end, using the same font drawn at 50× size in Chromium.
