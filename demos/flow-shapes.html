flow() around photos that are not plain boxes

The page is 4 columns, 640 × 760 px at 12 px, not the A3 grid. flow() takes rectangles, so each shape is sliced into one rectangle per line of text: its widest point inside that line, plus an 8 px margin.

Each row shows one shape twice. Left, before: the Phase 0 engine (commit e797b6d). Text takes only the wider side of a split line, gaps under 6 em stay empty, and every line is justified. Right, after: today's packages/engine/src/flow.js (D-037).

  • Gaps. Every gap of at least 3 em takes text, left to right.
  • Alignment applies only to shaped objects (round, rotated, free-form). A line with the shape on its right is flush left (align: 'left'); with the shape on its left, flush right (align: 'right'). Lines beside plain rectangles stay justified.
  • Other lines stay justified the export way (word spacing only, D-006).

Still open: each line clears the shape's widest point inside it, so the top and bottom of the round photo leave small wedges. A finer outline is later work.
