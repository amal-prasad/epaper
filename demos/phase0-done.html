  • Export module, with the reviewer's six conditions (rule 16):
    1. rewrite content streams with a real PDF tool;
    2. fail on Ghostscript warnings and on leftover neutrals;
    3. check every export's plates for K only and tone;
    4. map tints through the printer's K curve, and keep white out of the rewrite;
    5. set photo quality explicitly;
    6. output PDF/X with bleed.
