## Cut-lines (drop in this order when behind)

- Phase 0: `flow.png`.
- Phase 1: e-paper reader (send page images) → proof link (send PDF on WhatsApp). Never drop press export or backup.
- Phase 2: share cards → article map.

## Week-12 decision

- Go to software (Phase 3): ≥ 3 paying customers, B.minutes ≤ 90, ≥ 1 owner or operator asks to self-serve, hours after week 12 confirmed.
- Stay services: paying customers exist but B.minutes > 90 or nobody asks to self-serve; re-decide at week 20.
- Stop or pivot: 0 paying after a clean free issue to ≥ 4 qualified prospects, or B.minutes > 150.

Record the outcome as a decision-log row in `CLAUDE.md`.
