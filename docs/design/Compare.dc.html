Where you are better

  • Only tool here doing real text wrap, press-ready CMYK and hosted e-paper together
  • One file for print and e-paper: no re-making the paper twice
  • Kruti Dev converter removes a daily chore
  • Flat price beats XLR8’s per-page fee, most of all for dailies

Where you are worse

  • No track record; ePaper Designer has 50K+ installs
  • The ₹15 e-paper tier is thin-margin; ePaper Designer can match it, so it must lead into Press Pro
  • Fewer templates and one language at launch
  • No auto-placement until v2, which XLR8 already sells

The one-line pitch

जो फ़ाइल ई-पेपर बनती है, वही प्रेस में छपे — बिना ऑपरेटर के।

The file that becomes your e-paper is the one the press prints — no operator needed.
